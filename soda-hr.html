01 · What was happening

Benefits setup ran through brokers.

SME owners had no way to self-serve. Setting up medical and flex benefit plans meant manual back-and-forth with a broker, and it was error-prone at every step.

02 · What changed

One policy, fully self-serve.

SODA lets HR teams define policies, create plans and manage employees directly — one policy, one price, unlimited plans and member groups inside.

03 · System behaviour

Every company starts somewhere different.

New signups, teams switching off a broker, and companies with several member groups all move through the same guided, policy-first setup.

New companyGuided policy setupOne policy, unlimited plansTeam live

05 · Interaction craft

Details that built confidence, not just completion.

06 · Friction as data

Real usage found what testing didn't.

Live usage surfaced friction in plan setup, policy-change communication, and moments where HR loses confidence mid-flow — each one now feeds the next iteration, not a support ticket.

07 · What happened afterward

Product-market fit exists — the gap was reach, not capability.

10k+employees onboarded through the portal
40+companies onboarded in the first 3 months

The portal is the operational core; the work now is connecting it to how companies find and trust SODA — insurance acquisition is relationship-first, and the product has to earn that trust before features matter.
