The premise

Movement leaves a pattern.

The experience watches how you move an ambient orb—not who you are or what you type. That movement becomes the input for a short, hand-written reading.

How 160 readings are made

Four simple signals.
No mystery maths.

014 visual fields

The shader you choose sets the emotional family.

025 speed tiers

How quickly you move measures engagement intensity.

032 movement qualities

Focused or scattered entropy captures the character of your motion.

044 times of day

Dawn, day, dusk or night gives the reading a temporal layer.

4×5×2×4= 160 outcomes

Nothing here is random. The same movement at the same time of day always gives the same reading, and every line is written by hand, not generated.

Creative direction

Personal, without being invasive. Poetic, without being vague.

The finished experience should feel like a small ritual worth repeating. Time of day and how you move both change the reading, so it stays interesting on replay.

  • Mobile input through touch pressure and movement
  • A shareable result card with image export
  • Seasonal or lunar time layers
  • Sound response using archetype-led oscillator tones
